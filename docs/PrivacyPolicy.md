# Privacy Policy for Gym Bro

**Last Updated:** September 2026

Gym Bro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Gym Bro mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Data

When you create an account, we may collect:

- Email address
- Password (securely hashed)
- Basic profile information (such as your name or display name)

### B. Health and Fitness Data (Apple HealthKit)

With your explicit permission, the App reads and writes data to the Apple Health app (HealthKit) on your device.

- **Reading Data:** We read your daily steps and active energy burned to display your activity within your personal dashboard.
- **Writing Data:** We write your completed workouts to your Activity rings so your fitness progress is synced across your Apple devices.

**IMPORTANT:** We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your HealthKit data to third parties. Your HealthKit data is used solely to provide and improve the health and fitness tracking features within the App. It is not used for advertising or marketing purposes.

### C. Usage Data

We may collect automatically generated information about your usage of the App, such as crash logs and performance diagnostics, to help us improve the App's stability.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain the App.
- Authenticate your account and secure your data.
- Display your personal fitness analytics and progression charts.
- Improve our application by analyzing aggregated, anonymized usage trends.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

Your personal account data and workout logs are stored securely using Supabase, which employs robust industry-standard encryption and Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure that only you can access your personal data.

## 4. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access, update, or delete your personal information directly within the App.
- Revoke HealthKit permissions at any time via your device's Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Gym Bro.
